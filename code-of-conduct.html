POBSC Code of Conduct

Affiliated with ENYYSA & LIJSL. Applies to players, parents / guardians, and coaches.

Purpose

This Code of Conduct outlines the expectations for behavior on and off the field. It is designed to promote a safe, respectful, and positive environment for everyone involved in youth soccer — players, coaches, parents, referees, and spectators.

By joining our club, all players, parents/guardians, and coaches agree to uphold the standards of the Eastern New York Youth Soccer Association (ENYYSA) and the Long Island Junior Soccer League (LIJSL), in addition to the club-specific expectations below.

Player Code of Conduct

As a player, I will:

Respect the Game

  • Follow the rules and the spirit of the game.
  • Accept referee decisions without arguing or complaining.
  • Play fairly, without cheating, diving, or taunting.

Respect Others

  • Treat coaches, teammates, opponents, and referees with respect at all times.
  • Avoid foul language, bullying, or disrespectful behavior — on or off the field, including on social media.

Give My Best Effort

  • Arrive on time, prepared, and ready to play.
  • Work hard at practice and in games.
  • Stay focused, coachable, and positive regardless of the score.

Show Good Sportsmanship

  • Congratulate teammates and opponents.
  • Never gloat when winning or sulk when losing.
  • Represent my team and club with pride and humility.

Parent / Guardian Code of Conduct

As a parent or guardian, I will:

Encourage Positive Play

  • Support my child and the team in a positive way.
  • Applaud effort and improvement over just winning.
  • Let coaches coach — refrain from giving instructions from the sidelines.

Respect Officials, Coaches & Opponents

  • Never confront referees, opposing coaches, players, or parents.
  • Communicate concerns privately and respectfully with club staff when appropriate.
  • Trust that referees and coaches are doing their best for the game and the kids.

Be a Role Model

  • Avoid yelling, profanity, or negative comments.
  • Demonstrate composure, integrity, and patience — especially in challenging moments.
  • Encourage teamwork, respect, and fair play.

Support My Child’s Well-Being

  • Make sure my child is on time, properly equipped, and ready to participate.
  • Promote rest, proper nutrition, and prompt injury reporting.
  • Understand that soccer is for the players — not for the parents.

Coach Code of Conduct

As a coach, I will:

Prioritize Player Development

  • Put the long-term growth, skill-building, and enjoyment of every player ahead of short-term results.
  • Give players meaningful playing time and opportunities to develop in a variety of positions.

Treat Every Player Fairly

  • Apply the same standards, playing time philosophy, and discipline to all players without favoritism.
  • Respect each player’s individual pace of development and personal circumstances.

Communicate Openly with Families

  • Share clear, timely information about schedules, expectations, and player progress.
  • Listen to parent and player concerns respectfully, and address them promptly and constructively.

Foster Respect, Discipline, and Teamwork

  • Set clear standards of behavior and hold players accountable in a fair, age-appropriate way.
  • Build a team culture centered on mutual respect, cooperation, and shared responsibility.

Serve as a Positive Role Model

  • Model composure, integrity, and good sportsmanship at all times, including toward officials and opponents.
  • Use encouraging, respectful language with players, families, referees, and opposing coaches.

Consequences for Violations

The club reserves the right to take appropriate disciplinary action if this Code is violated by a player, parent/guardian, or coach. Depending on the severity and frequency of the conduct, this may include:

  • A verbal or written warning
  • Suspension from practices or games
  • Removal from the team or club
  • Reporting to ENYYSA and/or LIJSL for further disciplinary review

Agreement

By participating in this club, players, parents/guardians, and coaches agree to follow this Code of Conduct and uphold the values of respect, responsibility, sportsmanship, and safety.

A signed acknowledgment of this Code of Conduct is collected from each player, parent/guardian, and coach as part of the annual registration process.
