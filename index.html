Become a sponsor

POBSC is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Sponsor donations keep fees low, fund scholarships, and helped put AEDs at every field our children play on. A $500 donation puts your company's name or logo on a Little Nuggets, Pre-K or K division shirt and on our website.
